These references illuminate the ideas in the book. They come mostly from management and software; applying them to another situation means examining their context.

- 1911. Frederick Winslow Taylor, *The Principles of Scientific Management*. [Full text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/6435/pg6435-images.html)

- 1954. Peter Drucker, *The Practice of Management*. [Library record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb37570872q)

- 1960. Theodore Levitt, *Marketing Myopia*. [The reissue, at the publisher](https://store.hbr.org/product/marketing-myopia-harvard-business-review-classics/2601)

- 1968. Melvin Conway, *How Do Committees Invent?*. [Introduction and text by the author](https://www.melconway.com/Home/Conways_Law.html)

- 1975. Fred Brooks, *The Mythical Man-Month*. [Anniversary edition at the publisher](https://www.pearson.com/en-gb/subject-catalog/p/Brooks-Mythical-Man-Month-The-Essays-on-Software-Engineering-Anniversary-Edition-2nd-Edition/P200000000149/undefined)

- 1977. Michel Crozier and Erhard Friedberg, *L'Acteur et le Système*. [Record for the original edition](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34708065n)

- 1979. Donald T. Campbell, *Assessing the Impact of Planned Social Change*. [Article and DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0149-7189(79)90048-X)

- 1982. W. Edwards Deming, *Out of the Crisis*. [At the publisher](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262350037/out-of-the-crisis/)

- 1983. Andrew S. Grove, *High Output Management*. [At the publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/72467/high-output-management-by-andrew-s-grove-former-chairman-and-ceo-of-intel/)

- 1986. Richard Hamming, *You and Your Research*. [Transcript of the talk](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~robins/YouAndYourResearch.html)

- 1992. Bruno Latour, *Aramis, or the Love of Technology*. [The French edition at the publisher](https://www.editionsladecouverte.fr/aramis_ou_l_amour_des_techniques-9782707121202)

- 1997. Eric S. Raymond, *The Cathedral and the Bazaar*. [The author's text, revised since its first release](https://www.catb.org/esr/writings/cathedral-bazaar/cathedral-bazaar/)

- 1999. Amy Edmondson, *Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams*. [Article in the Harvard repository](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/13a7b031-0fdd-45ec-a7e0-2b80e2bc679f)

- Peter Norvig, *Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years*. [The author's text](https://www.norvig.com/21-days.html)

- 2011. Rich Hickey, *Simple Made Easy*. [Talk and notes](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/Simple-Made-Easy/)

- 2013. Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*. [The book's site](https://www.momtestbook.com/)

- 2017. Marty Cagan, *Inspired*, second edition. [The author on this edition](https://www.svpg.com/inspired-v2/)

- 2018. Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble and Gene Kim, *Accelerate*. [Introduction and extracts at the publisher](https://itrevolution.com/product/accelerate/)
