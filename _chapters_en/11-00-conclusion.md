A builder sees a problem, tries something, looks at what it changes, and goes again. You can start today, on a small scope.

Understand a problem before choosing a solution.
Try fast, with a one-hour version.
Put your work in the hands of the people it is for.
Come back to see what it changed, including when it disappoints.
Make next time easier.
Grow someone else.
Write down what you learned, and publish it.

These practices do not trace a single career. They grow together, in a job, a company, a community group or a personal project. And they have never been easier to reach: with today's tools, AI first among them, anyone can build far beyond their original trade.

## When two cards pull in different directions

Ship fast or check again. Take the initiative or wait for a decision. Remove a step or keep it. The cards do not always give the same answer. Three questions settle it almost every time.

1. **Can it be undone?** If yes, try. If not, take the time to check and to ask.
2. **Who pays if it fails?** If it is someone other than you, talk to them first.
3. **Which fact would change your mind?** Write it down, and look at it on the planned date.

## When the result disappoints

A failed attempt teaches you something, as long as you look at why: the assumption, the means, the execution, the context. Then decide: carry on, change, or stop. Stopping is a result, not a failure.

If your attempt caused damage, repair first, tell the people who need to know, then look for what was missing. If a published resource misleads people, correct it and say so.

## Passing on what helps

What you have learned only serves you while you keep it. Explain it, train someone, write it down, publish it. Start from your first attempts, stating their limits. The next builder may be looking for exactly what you know.

## Choosing what comes next

If you are starting out, begin with a person to help and a [useful first attempt](/first-try/). If you already practise, choose a strength to deepen or a precise difficulty, as in [Improving without starting over](/improving-without-starting-over/).

For a group, propose a [session](/workshop/). To back someone, ask what help would let them try. The [templates](/templates/) serve both.

The [four paths](/paths/) and the [index by situation](/by-situation/) give you a direct way in. The [builder test](https://build-here.africa/builder-test/) gives you a level from 1 to 5 and one practice to try. You do not have to finish the book to start.

**Choose one thing to try this week. Do it, then come back and see what it changed.**
