You get handed a task. The work is the problem behind it.

You're asked to update an invitation. Reading it through, you see the venue is there, but not how to get in. The task takes five minutes. The problem will send people to the wrong door.

Autonomy means moving forward without every step spelled out: understanding the why, proposing what comes next, deciding what's within your remit, flagging fast what isn't.

It doesn't mean working alone. Staying stuck without saying so is a decision too.

**To start:** before your next task, ask what it's for. If you can't explain it, you're not ready to do it.

**To go further:** bring the problem with a proposal, not just a question.

**If you're growing a team:** make clear what belongs to the team. A roadmap nobody may refuse is a queue. The ⇄ cards cover this.

**A sign it's working:** you can state the goal, your next action, and what you're missing to carry it out.

**Next:** understanding, to see what happens before and after your work.
