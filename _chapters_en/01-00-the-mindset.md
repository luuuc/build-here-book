A builder starts by noticing.

The same question comes back after every sign-up. Everyone answers it. You wonder why it keeps coming back.

That's the starting point: seeing a problem others work around, and wanting to understand it.

This section is about what makes that possible: asking the naive question straight away, saying "I don't know", changing your mind fast, defending an idea without defending your ego.

None of these are personality traits. They're habits. You can train them.

**To start:** ask today the question you've been keeping to yourself for a week.

**To go further:** go back to the last opinion you held on to for too long. What would have changed your mind sooner?

**If you're growing a team:** how you react to the first piece of bad news sets the rule for every one after it. The ⇄ cards cover this.

**A sign it's working:** you change your mind faster, and people bring you problems earlier.

**Next:** the craft, to turn what you notice into solid work.
