You learn fast here. The ground doesn't forgive much.

**The problems are close.**

The people you serve are a street, a call, a message away.

You see what's stuck without going through three layers of middlemen.

Feedback comes in days, not quarters.

**The needs are real.**

Payments, logistics, health, public services, education: a lot is still waiting to be built.

You don't have to invent a problem to justify your work.

What you ship changes something for someone you can go and see.

**Constraints force simplicity.**

Tight budgets, small teams, patchy connections, little room for error.

A service, an organisation or a tool that holds up under these conditions is tougher than most of what gets built with every comfort.

That's craft. And it travels.

**Your market can be local. Your level can't.**

Books, courses, communities, the best people in your field: all within reach from here.

You can serve your city and measure yourself against the world.

**The reference spot is open.**

Much of what gets done here has never been written down.

Whoever explains how they solved a local problem becomes the reference the next people were looking for.

<br>
"There's no ecosystem."

An ecosystem is builders who ship, publish and train the next ones. Every one of those moves is part of it.

"The best people leave."

Some do. What they passed on stays, and many keep the link. Build what outlasts departures.

"The references are elsewhere."

Take them from wherever they come. Their worth gets proven here.
