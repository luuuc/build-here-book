Choose the template that helps your next decision. You do not have to fill them all in. Delete a field that serves no purpose in your case; keep the limits, the agreements and the next step that matter. Use a space suited to the information you are allowed to share.

Worked examples are in [A useful first attempt](/en/first-try/), [Improving without starting over](/en/improving-without-starting-over/) and [Six weeks to learn together](/en/learning-as-a-team/). All their results are fictional.

## A bounded attempt
{: #template-bounded-attempt }

- **Person or use to help:** ...
- **Fact observed, and context:** ...
- **Assumption to examine:** ...
- **Smallest useful attempt:** ...
- **What already works and has to be preserved:** ...
- **Agreements, access and support needed; who confirms them:** ...
- **Time available and work displaced:** ...
- **Limits and the condition for stopping:** ...
- **Observation to gather, by whom, and when:** ...
- **After the attempt: fact observed / interpretation / unknown:** ...
- **Decision to carry on, change or stop; next responsibility:** ...

An attempt can confirm a practice that is already solid. It does not have to start from a flaw or end with something new.

## A decision note
{: #template-decision-note }

- **Decision, owner and date:** ...
- **Goal and realistic options, including keeping what exists:** ...
- **Information available and sources you can reach:** ...
- **Assumptions, unknowns and constraints:** ...
- **Choice and reason:** ...
- **Costs of preparing, using and maintaining:** ...
- **People affected and agreements obtained:** ...
- **Protections, the way back, and the limits of the mandate:** ...
- **The fact or the moment that triggers a review:** ...
- **At the review: result, limits of the observation, and next step:** ...

Keep the original reasoning. Add the feedback afterwards without rewriting what was known at the time.

## A before and after
{: #template-before-after }

| Point to examine | Before | After the attempt |
|---|---|---|
| Route taken and people involved | ... | ... |
| Useful result, or protection to preserve | ... | ... |
| Difficulty, rework or effort observed | ... | ... |
| Load moved onto someone else | ... | ... |
| Period, cases, and method of observation | ... | ... |
| Limits and alternative explanations | ... | ... |

**Decision and next check:** ...

If the situations differ, say so. You can describe a qualitative change without manufacturing a percentage. Fewer requests can mean fewer problems or less access to help.

## A handover that was accepted
{: #template-accepted-handover }

- **What is finished and what stays open:** ...
- **Remit handed over, and to whom:** ...
- **Resources, access and demonstration needed:** ...
- **What the recipient accepts, confirmed by them:** ...
- **Decisions that stay elsewhere, and who to ask:** ...
- **What to do if the handover gets stuck or someone is away:** ...
- **Who will check the final result, if needed, and when:** ...
- **End of the handing person's commitment:** ...

Sending a document is not enough to confirm someone picked it up. If nobody can accept, get the remit or the deadline ruled on rather than keeping an open-ended responsibility.

## Precise backing
{: #template-precise-backing }

- **Help the person is asking for:** ...
- **What I can offer inside my role:** ...
- **Time, means, date and agreements needed:** ...
- **What this help does not cover:** ...
- **What I am deferring to honour this:** ...
- **How the person can refuse or adjust the help:** ...
- **Agreed feedback: did the help happen, and what did it make possible?** ...
- **End, continuation or change to the agreement:** ...

An introduction needs agreement on both sides. A review can be useful without granting the power to approve the content or the business decision.

## A log of decisions and results
{: #template-decision-log }

| Moment and observation | Decision and reason | Owner, agreement and next check-in |
|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... |

Keep a line when a fact changes or confirms a decision. Do not create a daily record if the rhythm of the activity does not call for it. Write "not observable yet", "access missing" or "attempt paused" too, when that is the real situation.

## A team account
{: #template-team-account }

- **Period and question examined:** ...
- **What already worked and was kept:** ...
- **Actions taken:** ...
- **Effects observed, with their context:** ...
- **What is still unknown or not comparable:** ...
- **Real effort, help received, and work displaced:** ...
- **What each person wants to continue or adjust:** ...
- **Decision: keep / change / stop / check another way:** ...
- **Responsibilities accepted and means confirmed:** ...
- **Next useful check-in, or close the tracking:** ...

Six weeks is one possible period, as in the example; choose a moment when relevant feedback can exist. If the tracking itself gets too heavy, lighten it while keeping the checks that are needed.
