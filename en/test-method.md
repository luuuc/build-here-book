The test invites you to look at how you build today and to choose a practice to explore. Its headline asks a question, but its result does not decide whether you are a builder. You can also [go straight to a path](/en/paths/) without answering anything.

## Ten steps, six statements each

The ten steps match the ten capabilities in the book. Each screen shows six statements about concrete moves. Answer thinking about what you do today, in study, in a job, in a community group, on a personal project, or in helping people out. You have nothing to write in and nothing to publish.

Each statement offers six positions, from "Strongly disagree" to "Strongly agree". The scale has no neutral position: if you have not met the situation yet, choose "I haven't met this situation yet", which sits outside the scale. If time, access, an agreement or backing was missing, choose "I didn't have the conditions to try". You can go back to earlier steps and change your answers. All six answers in a step are needed to carry on.

## What the result shows

For each capability, the test separates the moves you recognise in your practice, the ones you recognise less, and the ones where your answers vary. It averages the positions chosen for that subject: from 4.5 up it suggests going deeper; up to 2.5 it suggests revisiting a practice; in between, it invites you to explore a concrete case. When fewer than three statements got a position on the scale, it suggests discovering the subject instead. Situations not met and missing conditions are shown separately and count as neither agreement nor disagreement. If you flag missing conditions, you can choose a line of work to clarify them. There is no overall score, no personality type, and no threshold that decides your identity.

These groupings are simple editorial bearings based on what you said. They do not prove a competence and do not establish that one practice is better than another in every situation. Choose the subject that interests you yourself. The test then asks whether you want to start, deepen your practice, act with a group, or back other builders. The line of work offers a bounded move, conditions to examine, and three cards from the book.

## Your answers and the limits of the test

The answers stay in this page's memory. They disappear when you leave or reload it, and they are not sent to the evaluation service. You can copy the line of work you chose; your other answers are not included. Old results from the scored questionnaire are neither restored nor converted.

The test has not been validated as a measurement instrument. Answers depend on the context you pick, on memory, and on the means available. It is not for hiring, for ranking colleagues, or for certifying a team. To act with other people, check their agreement, the time available, and what the attempt would displace. A proposal can also be cut back, deferred, or stopped.
