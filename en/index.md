## Where this stands

The book was written in French and is being translated card by card. What is
translated lives under `/en/`. What is not yet translated stays on the
[French edition](/livre/), which is complete and free to read.

## What the book is

A builder sets out to improve a concrete situation, watches what their action
produces, and learns from it for next time. You do not need a title, a team or
something already shipped to start.

The book is a set of short cards. Each one gives a reflex you probably have, the
reflex a builder reaches for instead, why it matters, and something to try this
week. You read the one that matches your situation, not the whole thing in order.
